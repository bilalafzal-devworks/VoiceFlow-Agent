Northwind Studio Employee Handbook (sample document for VoiceFlow tests)

Remote work policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Pakistan Standard Time. Remote workers must be reachable on Slack during core hours. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head.

Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave, up to 5 days, can be carried over to the next year. Sick leave is 10 days per year and requires a medical note after 3 consecutive days.

Equipment and expenses
New employees receive a laptop within their first week. Expenses above 50 USD need manager approval before purchase. Receipts must be submitted within 14 days through the finance portal.

Security rules
All company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 180 days. Report lost devices to the IT desk within 24 hours.

Onboarding
Week one covers tools setup and security training. Each new hire is assigned a buddy for the first 30 days. A 30-60-90 day plan is agreed with the manager in week two.
